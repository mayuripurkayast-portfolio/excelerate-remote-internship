## 📌 Project Overview
**Project Title:** *Making an Impact with Data: Uncovering DePaul's Global Student Pipeline*  
**Objective:** Transform unstructured, messy international admissions data into a structured format and an interactive **Power BI** dashboard to uncover demographic patterns, program preferences, intake trends, and provide data-driven recruitment recommendations.

---

## 🗓️ Weekly Milestones & Deliverables

### 📁 Week 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Data Profiling
* Profiled raw admissions dataset containing **7,543 records and 80 columns**.
* Detected critical data quality issues: 371K+ missing values, integer-stored dates, duplicated IDs (up to 48 repeats per ID), and inconsistent formatting.
* Formulated an initial Data Dictionary to standardize variable definitions across the team.

📄 **[View Week 1 EDA Report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sNVJum9mMqPFapIQUN856XbmCz6J6ex5/view?usp=sharing)**

---

### 📁 Week 2: Advanced Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
* Eliminated **2,543 duplicate records**, isolating **5,000 unique applicants**.
* Reduced column bloat from **80 columns down to 36 clean features**.
* Handled missing values using interpolation, standardized datetime fields, and extracted merged college/degree metadata into 3 structured columns.
* Documented transformation steps in a Data Cleaning Log.

📄 **[View Week 2 Cleaning Documentation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jw5P9MTXDlL8ZwXndFeQq0wG1RlybNBE/view?usp=sharing)**

---

### 📁 Week 3: Statistical Analysis & Metric Modeling
* Performed descriptive statistics, frequency distribution, and skewness checks across 95 countries and 19 academic programs.
* Established key admission funnel metrics: Total Applicants (5K), Total Admitted (3.2K), and Overall Admission Rate (65%).
* Segmented conversion funnels by Intake Year (2022–2024), Quarter, and College tier.

📄 **[View Week 3 Statistical Report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13iZ67ODmvyOGpXa06LIA_0cxScHJ1ltm/view?usp=sharing)**

### 📁 Week 4: Interactive Power BI Dashboard & Final Delivery
* Designed a multi-page interactive **Power BI Dashboard** with dynamic filters for Country, Program, College, and Intake Year.
* Synthesized key insights and strategic recommendations to improve global student diversity and enrollment balance.
* Delivered the final stakeholder slide deck and project presentation.

## 💡 Key Findings & Strategic Insights

| Dimension | Key Insight | Impact / Actionable Takeaway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Geographic Source** | **India represents 59%** of the applicant pool; growing pipeline from Nigeria & Ghana. | High reliance on a single region; need geographic diversification. |
| **Program Demand** | MS in **Computer Science & Data Science** drive the highest application volume. | STEM programs dominate; non-STEM programs need targeted marketing. |
| **Intake Pattern** | **Fall Intake** consistently accounts for the vast majority of applications and enrollments. | Admissions processing and marketing resources should center around Fall cycles. |
| **Funnel Metric** | Institutional admission rate stood at **65%** across 5,000 unique candidates. | Strong baseline conversion with room to optimize feeder partnerships. |

---

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Diversify Global Outreach:** Expand recruitment campaigns to secondary emerging markets to reduce dependence on single-country pipelines.
2. **Strengthen Feeder Partnerships:** Engage high-conversion international feeder institutions with customized scholarship programs.
3. **Resource Allocation:** Concentrate admissions counseling and processing teams around the peak Fall intake window.
4. **Promote Non-STEM Programs:** Run dedicated marketing for underrepresented colleges (Business, Liberal Arts) to balance enrollment.
